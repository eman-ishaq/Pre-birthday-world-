Main is kahani ko sirf dates ke saath nahi likh sakti. Kuch dates calendar par hoti hain, aur kuch dil par.

12 January 2024 — 7:00 PM

Mujhe aaj bhi woh shaam yaad hai. Main noodles bana rahi thi aur Snapchat streak bhej rahi thi. Phir achanak light chali gayi, Wi‑Fi disconnect ho gaya. Maine mobile data on kiya aur bina soche snap bhej diya… aur pata nahi kaise woh aapko chala gaya.

Phir screen par “Assalamualaikum” aaya. Maine “Walaikum Assalam” likha. Uske baad ek simple sa sawaal: “Aap kon?” Maine bhi wahi pooch liya. Phir woh ajeeb sa jawab — “Aap wohi jo main samajh raha hu.” Maine bhi likh diya, “Aap wohi jo main samajh rahi hu.”

Naam saamne aaye… aur phir pata nahi kaise ek random conversation ne meri routine mein jagah bana li. Pehle bas baat hui. Phir roz baat hone lagi. Kabhi hansi, kabhi gussa, kabhi choti si nok-jhok. Dosti dheere dheere itni gehri hoti gayi ke mujhe khud samajh nahi aaya ke kab ek normal conversation meri favourite notification ban gayi.

From Friends to Something More

Humne sirf achay din nahi dekhe. Laraiyan bhi hui, narazgiyan bhi, kabhi ek dusre se irritate bhi hue. Lekin ajeeb baat yeh thi ke har dafa baat phir wahi aa kar rukti — hum ek dusre ko khona nahi chahte thay. Dosti best-friend wali jagah tak pohanchi, aur shayad dil us se bhi aage nikal chuka tha.

8 May 2024 — The Page I Couldn’t Hide

Us din mera Urdu ka paper tha aur main tayari kar rahi thi. Ek rough page par maine “Eman” bohat dafa likha. Aur phir pata nahi kis himmat se, aapka naam teen jagah chhupa diya. Maine us page ki photo bheji aur kaha ke dekhein, aapka naam dhoondhein.

Aapne dhoond liya. Phir maine poocha ke ab mujhe kya karna chahiye. Pehle baat taal di gayi. Maine dobara poocha. Phir woh baat saamne aa hi gayi jo shayad hum dono ke dil mein pehle se thi — hamari dosti aage barh rahi thi, aur feelings bhi.

Main kuch der bilkul khamosh rahi. Mere liye yeh koi choti baat nahi thi. Maine kaha ke main Ammi aur Abu ke baghair aisa faisla nahi kar sakti. Aapne mujhe tasalli di ke baat samjhaane ki koshish ki jayegi. Aur us din meri zindagi mein ek naya lafz sirf lafz nahi raha — mohabbat ban gaya.

9 July 2024 — Woh Choti Si Raat

Jab ghar walay Punjab mein thay aur Karachi mein sirf main aur Urooj thay, ek raat mulaqat hui. Raat 12 se 1 ke darmiyan. Maine aapke liye chai banayi. Aapne woh chai pi. Hum paas kharay baat karte rahe. Bahar se dekha jaye to shayad woh sirf ek choti si mulaqat thi… lekin mere liye us raat ka har minute alag tha.

10 July ki subah phir mulaqat hui. 7–8 baje ka waqt tha. Neend se zyada baat karne ka shauq tha. Thori dair baad ghar walay Punjab se wapas aa gaye aur woh choti si azaadi khatam ho gayi, lekin woh dono mulaqatein mere dil mein reh gayin.

October 2024 — Ek Mahina Jo Yaad Ban Gaya

Ammi, Aliza aur Abu Punjab gaye aur taqreeban ek mahina aisa guzra jahan mulaqatein roz ki aadat ban gayin. Roz milna, baatein karna, hansna, waqt guzarna… aur do raatain bhi saath guzri jo mere liye kisi grand plan se zyada qeemti thin.

Mujhe un dinon ki ek khaas baat yaad hai: khushi ko kisi badi cheez ki zaroorat nahi thi. Bas aapka paas hona kaafi lagta tha.

31 March – 4 April 2025 — Chaar Din, Bohat Saari Yaadein

31 March ko ghar walay Punjab gaye. Main aur Urooj Karachi mein rahe kyun ke mere 9th class board exams thay. Mamu ki shaadi ke liye sab gaye thay, aur un chand dinon mein humein milne ka waqt mila.

2 April ko mera birthday tha. Celebration bohat chota tha, lekin mere liye bohat khaas. Kabhi kabhi badi decoration se zyada ek sincere effort yaad rehta hai.

3 April ko Urooj ke saath Imtiaz gaye. Chocolate mili, mera favourite juice mila. Ghar wapas aaye, baatein ki, raaton ko cheezein order ki, saath baithay. Aur phir 4 April… Punjab ki ticket, safar ka waqt, aur mere haath se khana khila kar woh rukhsat ka lamha. Kuch departures ajeeb hotay hain — insaan chala jata hai, lekin room mein uski presence bohat der tak mehsoos hoti rehti hai.

24 May 2026 — Train, Khamoshi Aur Nazar

24 May ko poora ghar train se Punjab gaya. Sab ke beech openly baat karna asaan nahi tha, is liye humne nazron ko apni zubaan bana liya. Kabhi ek nazar, kabhi ek choti si smile… aur dil samajh leta tha jo alfaaz keh nahi sakte thay.

Safar mein meri tabiyat kharab hui. Aap Sting aur nimco le aaye. Mujhe washroom tak do dafa saath le gaye kyun ke main dar rahi thi. Mujhe us waqt kisi dramatic sentence ki zaroorat nahi thi. Care khud ek sentence ban gayi thi.

Punjab — Raaten Jo 4:30 Tak Chali

Punjab pohanch kar cousins ke saath raat ki gatherings hoti rahin. Kabhi 4 baje, kabhi 4:30 tak. Ice cream, burgers, noodles, hasi, random conversations… woh raatain simple thin, magar unmein ek warmth thi jo mujhe ab bhi yaad hai.

Ek raat meri tabiyat phir kharab hui. Us raat mujhe sirf yeh yaad nahi ke kya khaya ya kya kaha gaya — mujhe yaad hai care. Body, shoulders, hands, feet aur head massage karna. Burger khilana. Aur mere liye chocolate la kar usay apne haath se khilana. Mere liye mohabbat ka matlab us raat kisi quote mein nahi tha. Woh mere saamne care ki shakal mein baithi thi.

Wapas Aane Se Pehle

Funville gaye. Kamal Chishti ke mazar par gaye. Hotel mein khana khaya. Aur wahan se mere liye ek bracelet bhi liya gaya. Bracelet chota sa tha, lekin meri yaad mein uski jagah bohat badi hai — kyun ke kuch cheezein price se nahi, moment se precious hoti hain.

13 June 2026 — Karachi Wapas

13 June ko Karachi wapas aa gayi. Phir zindagi apni normal speed par aayi… aur shayad hum dono ke andar bhi bohat kuch normal nahi raha. 20 June se 3 September tak laraiyan hoti rahin. Kuch baatein dil ko bohat gehra hurt kar gayin.

Main yeh sab is liye nahi likh rahi ke purani baaton ko dobara zinda karun. Main sirf itna chahti hoon ke meri khamoshi ko kabhi meri feelings ki kami na samjha jaye. Main larai nahi chahti. Main aapko annoy nahi karna chahti. Main sirf chahti hoon ke jab mere dil mein koi baat ho, usay samajhne ki thori si jagah mile.

Kabhi kabhi aansu kisi ki choice nahi hotay. Aur kabhi ek larki kisi relationship ki baat is liye khud start nahi karti ke usay bohat shauq hota hai — woh tab bolti hai jab usay lagta hai ke baat andar rakhne se aur zyada dard hoga. Maine bhi isi liye bola, kyun ke mujhe hamari parwah thi.

Allah sab dekhta hai. Shayad waqt ke saath kuch baatein aur kuch decisions zyada clearly samajh aayen. Main aaj bhi bad-dua nahi karti. Main chahti hoon ke aap kamyab hon, khush hon, sukoon mein hon. Aur agar meri kisi baat mein kabhi kami rahi ho, to meri niyyat ko samajhne ki koshish zaroor karna.

Still Us

Itni saari dates, itni saari fights, itni saari hansi… aur phir bhi ek simple si baat abhi tak sach hai: hum dost hain. Hum mohabbat karte hain. Aur mere dil mein abhi bhi yeh umeed hai ke hum ek dusre ko behtar samajhna seekh sakte hain.

Mujhe perfect kahani nahi chahiye. Mujhe woh kahani chahiye jahan dono log galtiyon ke baad ruk kar ek dusre ko samajhne ki koshish karein.

Agar kabhi aap is poori kahani ko dobara parhein, to please sirf dates mat parhna. Un dates ke darmiyan jo meri feelings thin… unhein bhi parhna.
