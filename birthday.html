A Birthday Made of Little Things

🎁

Gift #1 — A Dua

Allah aapko sehat, sukoon, kamyabi, barkat aur lambi khubsurat zindagi ata farmaye. Ameen.

Meri sab se pehli birthday wish: aapke dil ko woh sukoon mile jo duniya ki koi cheez khareed nahi sakti.
💌

Gift #2 — A Secret Note

Is box mein ek choti si baat hai jo main shayad roz nahi keh pati.

Aap meri life ki un memories mein se hain jinhein main delete nahi karna chahti — chahe chapter kitna bhi easy ya difficult raha ho.
🌙

Gift #3 — Midnight Wish

Raat ke kisi quiet moment ke liye.

Jab sab so rahe hon aur aap yeh page kholen, meri taraf se ek silent wish: Allah aapko har gham se aman de.
✨

Gift #4 — One Promise

Perfect promise nahi. Real promise.

Main apni taraf se koshish karungi ke feelings ko behtar words doon, aur misunderstandings ke darmiyan bhi respect ko jagah doon.
🕊️

Gift #5 — Peace

Sab se expensive gift, phir bhi free.

Meri dua hai ke aapki zindagi mein peace itna ho ke aapko har din kisi na kisi wajah se shukar aaye.
🎀

Gift #6 — A Memory

Ek memory choose karein…

12 January ki accidental snap se le kar aaj tak — kabhi socha nahi tha ek random message itni saari memories ka start ban jayega.
Birthday Letter

“Aapko advance birthday wish karna sirf is liye nahi ke main pehle kehna chahti hoon. Main is liye keh rahi hoon kyun ke mere dil ne kaha ke ek normal wish se kaam nahi chalega. Is liye yeh poori little world ban gayi. Aap isay jab bhi kholen, please ek baat yaad rakhein: meri taraf se sab se sincere wish aapki khushi, sehat aur sukoon hai.”
